# Hair Nova visual system

Blue-led beauty campaign with the warmth and directness of a local manufacturer. A salon buyer browsing on their phone in a bright shop should see the texture immediately and find the wholesale route easily.

Preserve the supplied Roboto Slab / Inter pairing and canonical wordmark. Steel blue #3077B2 and cyan #00ADDF lead; cream #F6F0E6 and charcoal #28221F support. Small blue text uses the darker steel-700 for contrast.

Use full-subject real product portraits, graphic blue planes, oversized slab headlines and small provenance details. Keep catalogue images square-edged and controls at 4px. The maker bento and craft photograph use softer 20px corners (16px on mobile). One large blue bento panel is paired with stacked warm neutral panels. The homepage moves from brand to collection to maker story to enquiry.

Keep interaction subtle: image zoom on hover, a sticky desktop craft photograph that follows the step nearest the viewport centre, and explicit process selectors on mobile. Desktop step descriptions remain visible. Never conceal essential first-screen content behind a reveal. Reduced motion removes movement and image crossfades.
