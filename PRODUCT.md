# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
South African salons, resellers and distributors sourcing hair, with consumers exploring textures as the secondary audience.

## Product Purpose
Generate wholesale enquiries and showcase the manufacturer's real catalogue. No checkout, prices, invented specifications or unsupported performance claims.

## Brand Personality
Warm, confident, craft-led. Family-owned and Asian-South African; manufacturing in Cosmo City, Johannesburg since 2009. These facts and the brand direction come from the supplied brand kit.

## Anti-references
The user rejects a clean but boring template. The brand kit rejects imitation Western luxury, scripts, pink beauty clichés and discount urgency.

## Design Principles
Lead with real hair photography. Make local origins visible. Bring products forward in the journey. Keep a clear route to wholesale enquiries. Preserve CMS content and functional forms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Follow the brand kit's AA text contrast guidance. Support keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion, readable mobile layouts and descriptive image alternatives.
